Common Questions

Bathroom Remodeling FAQs

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

A typical full bathroom remodel takes 2–3 weeks once materials are on site. We provide a written schedule with your estimate and keep you updated throughout.

Can I stay in my home during the remodel?

Yes, in most cases. We contain dust, protect finished areas, and keep at least one bathroom functional in multi-bath homes whenever possible.

Do you handle the plumbing and electrical work?

Yes — all trades are handled in-house or by our licensed trade partners, and we manage any required permits and inspections on your behalf.

What adds the most value to a bathroom remodel?

Walk-in showers, quality tilework, good lighting and water-efficient fixtures consistently deliver the strongest return — and the most daily enjoyment.

Do you offer a warranty?

Every bathroom remodel includes our 1-year workmanship warranty, plus all manufacturer warranties on fixtures and materials passed directly to you.
