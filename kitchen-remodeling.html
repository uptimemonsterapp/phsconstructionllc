Zoning & Permits

Understanding Permit Requirements for Kitchen Remodeling

It's often glossed over, but some kitchen remodels need a permit. Here's how to tell.

✓ Typically No Permit Required

  • Replacing countertops in-place (same footprint, no plumbing move)
  • Refacing or replacing cabinets with no structural or electrical changes
  • Painting, hardware updates and minor cosmetic work
  • Flooring replacement without subfloor structural work

⚠ Permit Required

  • Moving or adding plumbing — sink relocation, dishwasher rough-in, new water lines
  • Electrical changes — new circuits, moved outlets, recessed lighting rough-in
  • Removing or modifying walls, especially load-bearing walls
  • Ceiling work involving electrical or structural elements

There's no clear line between the two — our experienced team will tell you whether your project needs permits, and if it does, we handle the entire permit process for you.

Common Questions

Kitchen Remodeling FAQs

Common questions homeowners ask — answered directly.

Does my kitchen remodel require a permit?

It depends on the scope. A counter swap or new cabinets often don't; structural, plumbing or electrical work usually will. We'll tell you up front, and if a permit is needed, we take care of everything on your behalf.

What if a load-bearing wall needs to be removed?

Load-bearing wall removal requires a structural assessment, a permit, and staged inspections. We manage the structural evaluation and permit filing in-house as part of the project scope — never outsourced or left to you.

Do you offer itemized estimates?

Every estimate is fully itemized — labor, materials, permit fees and trade costs listed separately. You see the complete breakdown before you sign anything.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

A cabinet refacing can take just days; a full remodel with plumbing, flooring and electrical typically takes 4–6 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline with every estimate.

Can you coordinate my kitchen remodel with new flooring?

Absolutely — it's one of the most common combinations we deliver. Our kitchen flooring installation experts sequence the work so cabinets, counters and floors come together perfectly.
