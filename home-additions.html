Common Questions

Home Addition FAQs

How much does a home addition cost?

Costs depend on size, structure and finishes. A single-room addition typically starts around $40,000–$60,000, while second stories and ADUs range higher. We provide fully itemized written estimates before you commit to anything.

How long does a home addition take?

Most additions take 2–4 months of construction once permits are approved. We give you a realistic schedule up front — and we stick to it.

Will the addition match my existing home?

Yes. We match roofing, siding, windows and interior finishes so the addition looks like it was always part of your home, inside and out.

Do I need a permit for a home addition?

Almost always, yes — and we handle the entire permitting and inspection process for you, including any structural engineering documentation.

Can I live in my home during construction?

In most cases, yes. We sequence the work to keep your home secure and livable, and we advise honestly if a phase will be disruptive.

Does an addition add value to my home?

Well-designed additions — especially bedrooms, bathrooms and expanded kitchens — consistently deliver strong resale value in the NJ market, plus the daily value of space that fits your life.
