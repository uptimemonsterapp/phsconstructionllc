Common Questions

Flooring Installation FAQs

Do I need a permit for flooring installation?

In most cases, no — flooring replacement is considered cosmetic work. If structural subfloor repairs are needed, we'll advise you and handle any required paperwork.

Can you match existing flooring in an adjacent room?

Often, yes. We source matching materials and lace new hardwood into existing floors with expert sanding and finishing for a seamless look.

What happens if my subfloor has problems?

We test before we quote, so surprises are rare. If we find damage, we show you exactly what's needed and price the repair before proceeding.

How long does flooring installation take?

Most single rooms are completed in 1–2 days; whole-home projects typically take 3–5 days depending on material and prep work required.

Do you subcontract installation work?

No. Your floor is installed by PHS Construction's own experienced craftsmen — the same team from estimate to final walkthrough.
