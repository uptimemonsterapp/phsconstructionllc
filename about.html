Our Story

A Remodeling Contractor Built on Trust

PHS Construction has been providing high-quality residential construction services for homeowners for over 50 years. With innovative design, an eye for detail, and a commitment to quality products and service, we specialize in practical, beautiful remodeling work. We have transformed homes across the region — enhancing usability and design without ever losing that feeling of home.

We are a one-stop solution for all your home remodeling and construction needs, handling everything from the initial design to the final walkthrough. Our remodeling specialists work with you to create stunning designs that reflect your style, tastes and needs, bringing your dream home to life.

We focus exclusively on residential remodeling — kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, home additions, and flooring installation and renewal. It is this specialization that has allowed us to hone our skills, so we can take on any project with confidence.

Through our commitment to quality in all materials and workmanship, dedication to customer service, and a unique vision for creating beautiful spaces that enhance the way you live, we are proud to have earned a 5-star reputation within the community. With PHS, you get more than a contractor — you get a partner dedicated to creating the living space you deserve.
