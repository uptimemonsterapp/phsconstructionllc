FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We have answers. If you don't see yours below, give us a call.

What services does your construction company offer?

PHS Construction specializes in residential remodeling including bathroom remodeling, kitchen remodeling, flooring installation such as hardwood, tile, vinyl, laminate and carpet, and home additions such as second stories, ADUs, and sunroom additions.

How long does a remodeling project take?

Every project is different. A bathroom remodel usually takes 2–3 weeks, a kitchen remodel can take 4–6 weeks, and home additions can take 2–4 months. We always provide a clear schedule before any work begins — and we stick to it.

How much does home remodeling cost?

Costs vary widely depending on scope and materials. A bathroom remodel typically ranges from $8,000–$35,000, while a full kitchen remodel can range from $20,000–$80,000+. We provide detailed written estimates at no charge before you make any decisions.

Do you offer warranties on your work?

Yes. All PHS Construction projects come with a 1-year workmanship warranty. We also pass any manufacturer warranties on materials and fixtures installed in your home directly to you.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. We provide free, no-obligation estimates for any project. We'll visit your home, assess the project, and deliver a detailed written quote within 24–48 hours. Reach us at (732) 766-0503 or visit our office in Crosswicks, NJ.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. PHS Construction is fully licensed as a general contractor and carries comprehensive liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage on every project.
